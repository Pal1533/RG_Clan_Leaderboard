Privacy Policy

Community Clan Clash Cup run by Pal and JesusDied4U. Not the official game.

← Back to standings

This page covers the Clan Clash Cup site, the public player board, and the ATLAS HUD that writes scores. We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We keep what we need to show standings and stop spam.

What we store

  • Clan name, tag, membership, event baselines, and member MMR used for cup score.
  • In-game name and scores if you also appear on the player leaderboard.
  • A Firebase auth id from the HUD (anonymous sign-in) so a row belongs to one player.
  • A device id this browser keeps so we can count visits and rate-limit abuse. That id is not your name.
  • Admin Google account email if Pal or JesusDied4U sign in to manage the cup.

This browser may also keep small local notes (pinned clans, last-seen event) that never leave your device unless you clear site data.

Who can see it

Anyone can see public cup standings. Hosting and the database run on GitHub and Google Firebase, so those companies process the same records as part of providing those services. Admins can see extra ops data that is not shown on the public board.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade player or clan lists.
  • We do not ask for your Rocket Goal password.
  • We do not collect payment cards, government ids, or biometrics.
  • These pages are not directed at children under 13. If a parent thinks we listed a child, ping us and we will remove the row.

Ask us to take you off

Post in the Rocket Goal Championship Discord and ask Pal or JesusDied4U to delete your row or clan listing. Cached copies can take a little time to expire.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the date below.

This is a volunteer community project, not legal advice.
